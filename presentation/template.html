6 · Cross-validation and ensemble plan

  • Same folds for all three models: repeated stratified 5-fold (5 repeats = 25 fits per config), stratified on label × subscribed-in-2013-14. Each fold then holds ~10 of the hard new/lapsed positives.
  • Score on pooled out-of-fold AUROC averaged over repeats, not single-fold means: fold-to-fold spread is ~0.04, too noisy to tell 0.96 from 0.97. Models are compared pairwise on identical splits.
  • Tune on one 5-fold split, confirm on all 5 repeats. The top 3 configs per model get the full 5×5. Ties go to the simpler model.
  • Report two numbers per model: overall AUROC and AUROC on the hard group (accounts that did not subscribe in 2013-14). Gains there decide the top of the leaderboard but barely move the overall number.
  • All preprocessing fitted inside each training fold (imputation, scaling, encoders). No oversampling, since AUROC doesn't need balanced classes.
  • Rare signals (multiple.subs: 12 recent subscribers; 2014 gifts: 31 non-subscribers) stay as ordinary features. Regularisation and leaf-size limits stop models from memorising them.
  • Ablations, decided in advance: with vs without the ID-cohort group; with vs without don_first_in_2014; normal vs strict (pre14_) donation totals. A feature stays only if its gain beats the fold noise.
  • Optional time-shifted check: rebuild features as of 2012-13 and predict 2013-14 across all 19,833 accounts (982 positives). The pipeline automatically drops donation and ID features here because they can't be shifted. This is a stress test, not the selection metric.
  • Ensemble: start with an equal-weight average of the three models' out-of-fold probabilities. Learned weights or stacking only if evaluated with nested CV. Leaderboard submissions are sanity checks (10/day), not a tuning signal.
  • Cost: 84 configs × 5 folds, plus 9 finalists × 25 fits, on 6,941 rows: roughly 5–15 minutes on a laptop, with random forests the slowest.
