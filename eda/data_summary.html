    subscriptions.csv

    1 row per account × season
    • 28,627 rows, 6,267 accounts, 21 seasons (1993-94 to 2013-14). No row for 2014-15, which is the target.
    • Columns: package (Full, Quartet, Trio…), no.seats, location (venue series), section, price.level, subscription_tier, multiple.subs.
    • 32% of train accounts and 32% of test accounts appear here.
    • Volume has fallen from ~1,900 subscriptions per season in the late 1990s to ~1,000 now.

    concerts.csv & concerts_2014-15.csv

    programme metadata
    • concerts.csv: 103 performances of 27 programmes over 4 seasons (2010-11 to 2013-14). Each programme (set) repeats at several venues.
    • concerts_2014-15.csv: the 6 planned sets for the target season. No location column.
    • Free text (who, what) holds conductor, soloists and repertoire. Line breaks are \r; the 2014-15 file has mojibake quotes and accents (e.g. ÒVa tacitoÓ).
    • No account IDs here. Join to tickets_all.csv on season + set (+ location).

    zipcodes.csv

    1 row per US zip code
    • 42,522 US zip codes with City, State, Lat/Long, type, plus IRS-style TaxReturnsFiled, EstimatedPopulation, TotalWages (32% missing).
    • Zipcode is an integer, so leading zeros are lost. Pad to 5 digits before joining.
    • Account billing zips are sometimes ZIP+4 (94501-3315). Take the first 5 characters. of present billing zips then match.
    • Possible derived features: distance to each venue, local income (TotalWages / TaxReturnsFiled), population density.

    Data quality notes & gotchas

    Timing. first.donated runs to 2014-09-11 and amount.donated.lifetime may include 2014 gifts, i.e. around the time 2014-15 subscriptions were bought. Decide whether that is acceptable "known before the season" information. On the leaderboard it would apply to test accounts equally.
    • Encoding: account.csv is not valid UTF-8; use latin-1. concerts_2014-15.csv reads as UTF-8 but contains Mac-Roman mojibake (Ò Ó Õ). concerts.csv has a UTF-8 BOM.
    • Zip codes: mixed 5-digit, ZIP+4, and 4-digit (leading zero dropped) formats. 15% of accounts lack a billing zip, and none of the 980 train accounts without one subscribed.
    • Mixed types: tickets_all.price.level mixes numbers and labels. subscription_tier takes fractional values (0.5, 1.5…), which look like seat-weighted package size.
    • Missing: subscriptions.section 16%, price.level 12%. account.shipping.* 99%. relationship 97%.
    • Account universe: account.csv also holds ~9,900 accounts in neither train nor test. They are usable for unsupervised or aggregate features but have no labels.
    • Submission format: columns ID,Predicted, 2,975 rows, probabilities (not 0/1).
